# Design System Brief

## Design Goal

The site should feel:

- Technical.
- Trustworthy.
- Clean.
- Premium.
- Practical.
- Conversion-focused.
- Indian business friendly.

It should not feel:

- Generic digital marketing agency.
- Cyberpunk security brand.
- AI hype landing page.
- Founder-only personal portfolio.
- Spiritual/philosophy site.

## Visual Direction

Recommended theme:

> Smoky Jade + Business Clarity

The UI should support repeated scanning by serious buyers. Avoid oversized decorative sections and vague hero visuals. Use clear sections, proof, short copy, icon-supported service cards, comparison blocks, and audit CTAs.

## Color System

Primary:

- Midnight Teal `#112A33`: depth, footer, dark panels, and high-trust moments.
- Smoky Jade `#2E5966`: primary CTA, links, active states, and technical emphasis.
- Ocean Sage `#4E8A8F`: secondary accents, service accents, icons, and soft interaction states.
- Burnished Amber `#E0A458`: certification, proof, and premium highlights only.

Support:

- Porcelain `#FAF7F2` and white: readable sections and card contrast.
- Slate `#1F2937`: body text and serious content.
- Jade-tinted borders and dividers: soft structure without generic SaaS blue.
- Red or amber: issue/warning states, used sparingly.

Avoid:

- Enterprise navy + blue + gold styling.
- Dominant purple/blue gradients.
- One-color palette.
- Overly dark full-page UI.
- Excessive amber/gold that makes the site feel ceremonial rather than professional.

## Typography

Recommended:

- Headings: Avenir Next, Inter, or a similar crisp business sans.
- Body: Avenir Next, Inter, Kohinoor Devanagari, Noto Sans Devanagari, or similar readable multilingual sans.

Rules:

- No viewport-scaled font sizes.
- No negative letter spacing.
- Keep headings tight on service cards.
- Use larger type only for hero and major page titles.
- Body copy should be short and scannable.

## Image And Asset Direction

Use real assets where possible:

- Founder photo.
- Certification screenshots or verified credential cards.
- Website screenshots.
- Before/after examples.
- Blog illustrations only if they support the topic.

Avoid:

- Generic stock laptop images.
- Dark abstract cybersecurity imagery.
- Decorative gradient blobs.
- Fake dashboards with invented metrics.

For the Next.js build:

- Use `next/image`.
- Define stable aspect ratios.
- Use responsive image sizes.
- Avoid layout shift.
