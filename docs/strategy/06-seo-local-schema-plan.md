# SEO, Local, And Schema Plan

## SEO Goals

The site should help search engines and users understand:

- Who Chaitanya Media is.
- What services it provides.
- Where it serves.
- Why it can be trusted.
- How to contact the business.

The rebuild should prioritize crawlable HTML, static rendering where possible, clean URLs, unique metadata, internal links, and structured data.

## Metadata Rules

Every indexable page needs:

- Unique title.
- Unique meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph title and description.
- Open Graph image.
- Breadcrumb where useful.

Examples:

Home title:

> Chaitanya Media | Website, SEO & Security Partner in India

Home description:

> Secure, SEO-ready websites for Indian businesses. Chaitanya Media builds website infrastructure, Google visibility, and website protection for businesses that depend on trust and inquiries.

SEO service title:

> SEO & Google Visibility Services | Chaitanya Media

Security service title:

> Website Security & Malware Protection | Chaitanya Media

## Indexing Rules

Index:

- Home.
- Services.
- Service pages.
- Results.
- About.
- Founder.
- Blog index.
- Useful blog posts.
- Contact.
- Legal pages if needed.

Noindex:

- Thank-you page.
- Internal search pages.
- Any account or shop page if ecommerce is inactive.
- Prototype URLs.
- Thin duplicate pages.

Remove or redirect:

- `/founder/` current 404 should become a real page or redirect.
- `/shop/` if there is no active product catalog.
- `/my-account/` if ecommerce is not active.
- Duplicate security URLs should consolidate.

## Local SEO

Owner must confirm:

- Exact business name.
- Exact phone number.
- WhatsApp number.
- Email.
- Address or service-area model.
- Primary city: Mumbai, Badlapur, or both.
- Service areas: Badlapur, Ambernath, Kalyan, Dombivli, Navi Mumbai, Mumbai.
- GBP URL.
- Business hours.

Google Business Profile guidance emphasizes complete and accurate business information. The site should match GBP NAP data exactly.

Recommended local pages after launch:

- `/local-seo-mumbai/`
- `/wordpress-website-development-mumbai/`
- `/website-security-mumbai/`
- `/wordpress-malware-removal-mumbai/`

Do not create thin local pages. Each local page needs specific proof, FAQs, service relevance, and clear local intent.

## Structured Data

Global schema:

- Organization or LocalBusiness.
- WebSite.
- BreadcrumbList.

Founder page:

- Person.
- sameAs public profiles after owner verification.

Service pages:

- Service.
- FAQPage if FAQs are visible on the page.
- BreadcrumbList.

Blog posts:

- Article.
- Person author.
- BreadcrumbList.

Proof/results page:

- Organization.
- Person.
- ItemList for credentials or proof items if structured cleanly.

Do not add review schema unless reviews are first-party, visible on the page, and policy-compliant.
